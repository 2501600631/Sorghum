Fig. 1 | Whole grain prediction of four sorghum composition traits. Panels a–d show crude protein, moisture, crude ash and crude fibre, respectively. Each blue circle represents one of 98 coded samples and compares its recorded reference value with the arithmetic mean of its ten out-of-fold predictions from the nested whole grain workflow. The sample was excluded from the training partition used to generate each prediction. Light vertical intervals extend one sample standard deviation above and below the repeat-mean prediction (ten repeat predictions per sample; standard deviation calculated with nine degrees of freedom). These intervals describe split-to-split variation and are neither confidence intervals for the mean nor calibrated prediction intervals. The dashed gray line is the identity line. The R² and RMSE annotations are medians of the ten repeat-level performance estimates, each calculated using 98 held-out predictions; they are not metrics recomputed from the averaged points. All coded samples are shown, with no outcome-based exclusions or fitted trend line. Panels use trait-specific numerical ranges. Reference and predicted contents are expressed as percentages (%); RMSE is expressed in percentage points.
