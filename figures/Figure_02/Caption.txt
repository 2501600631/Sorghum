Fig. 2 | Repeated cross-validation errors for three measurement workflows. Panels a–d (top left, top right, bottom left and bottom right) correspond to crude protein, moisture, crude ash and crude fibre. Whole denotes the nested selection among eight whole grain model families; Assisted denotes the nested selection among four transfer families fitted with paired whole and ground training spectra but predicting from whole grain spectra only; Ground denotes the direct ground grain reference, selected among eight model families and requiring a ground spectrum at prediction. Each symbol is the RMSE of 98 held-out predictions assembled across the five outer folds of one repeat. Ten repeats are plotted per workflow and trait: blue circles, orange squares and gray triangles identify Whole, Assisted and Ground, respectively. Thin gray lines connect matched repeats. Boxes span the 25th–75th percentiles, the dark horizontal line is the median, and whiskers extend to the most extreme observed repeat value within 1.5 interquartile ranges of the box. All individual repeats, including those beyond the whiskers, are displayed. Horizontal offsets separate points and have no analytical meaning. Repeats share samples and training observations, so the distributions are descriptive rather than ten independent experiments. Numerical y-axis scales differ among panels. Paired whole-versus-assisted inferential analyses are given in Table 3; the figure itself does not assign statistical significance. RMSE is expressed in percentage points.
Reference concentrations and their predictions are expressed on an as-is (wet) basis. Concentration-error metrics are in percentage points of that basis.
